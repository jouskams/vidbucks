# Code of Conduct

We want Vidbucks to be a welcoming place to build things. Be kind, be specific, assume good faith.

**Expected:** respectful, constructive discussion; giving credit; accepting feedback gracefully; focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualised content, deliberately sharing others' private information, or pushing people to break copyright (for example asking the tool to download or re-upload other people's videos).

Maintainers may edit, reject or remove comments, commits and contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban people who repeatedly or seriously violate it. Report concerns through the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or by opening a confidential maintainer discussion on GitHub.
